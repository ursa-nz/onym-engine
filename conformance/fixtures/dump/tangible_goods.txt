term: tangible goods
[Definitions]
  - (noun) any type of good that can be physically touched, acquired to satisfy needs and desires.
      "A physical good may cost more than an intangible good, but is easier to return or track."
[Synonyms]
  - physical goods
[Is a kind of]
  - commodity, trade good, good
    - artifact, artefact
      - whole, unit
        - object, physical object
          - physical entity
            - entity
