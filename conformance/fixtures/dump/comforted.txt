term: comforted
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) made comfortable or more comfortable in a time of distress
      "the news make her feel comforted"
  - (verb) give moral or emotional strength to
      "The good news will comfort her"
  - (verb) lessen pain or discomfort; alleviate
      "ease the pain in your legs"
