term: snappy
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) apt to speak irritably
      "a snappish tone of voice"
  - (adjective) smart and fashionable
      "snappy conversation"
      "some sharp and whipping lines"
  - (adjective) pleasantly cold and invigorating
      "crisp clear nights and frosty mornings"
      "a nipping wind"
      "a nippy fall day"
      "snappy weather"
  - (adjective) marked by up-to-dateness in dress and manners
      "a dapper young man"
      "a jaunty red hat"
  - (adjective) quick and energetic
      "a brisk walk in the park"
      "a lively gait"
      "a merry chase"
      "traveling at a rattling rate"
      "a snappy pace"
      "a spanking breeze"
