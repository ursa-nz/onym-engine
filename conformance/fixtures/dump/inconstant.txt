term: inconstant
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) likely to change frequently often without apparent or cogent reason; variable
      "inconstant affections"
      "an inconstant lover"
      "swear not by...the inconstant moon- Shakespeare"
[Antonyms]
  - constant (direct)
      -> steadfast
      -> staunch
      -> unswerving
      -> unfailing
      -> unflagging
[Derived forms]
  - faithlessness
  - falseness
  - fickleness
  - inconstancy
  - changefulness
[Similar to]
  - false
  - untrue
  - fickle
  - volatile
[Attributes]
  - constancy
  - stability
