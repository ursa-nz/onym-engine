term: as
[Definitions]
  - (adverb) to the same degree (often followed by ‘as’)
      "they were equally beautiful"
      "birds were singing and the child sang as sweetly"
      "sang as sweetly as a nightingale"
      "he is every bit as mean as she is"
  - (noun) a very poisonous metallic element that has three allotropic forms; arsenic and arsenic compounds are used as herbicides and insecticides and in various alloys; found in arsenopyrite and orpiment and realgar
  - (noun) a United States territory on the eastern part of the island of Samoa
