term: wraithlike
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) lacking in substance
      "strange fancies of unreal and shadowy worlds"
      "dim shadowy forms"
      "a wraithlike column of smoke"
[Synonyms]
  - shadowy
[Antonyms]
  - insubstantial (indirect)
      -> substantial
      -> real
      -> material
[Similar to]
  - insubstantial
  - unsubstantial
  - unreal
