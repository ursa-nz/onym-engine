term: untempered
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) not brought to a proper consistency or hardness
      "untempered mortar"
      "untempered steel"
  - (adjective) not moderated or controlled
      "his untempered individualism"
[Synonyms]
  - unhardened
[Antonyms]
  - tempered (direct)
      -> treated
      -> hardened
      -> toughened
      -> curable
      -> sunbaked
[Similar to]
  - brittle
  - unannealed
  - unmoderated
