term: carbuncled
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) afflicted with or resembling a carbuncle
  - (adjective) set with carbuncles
[Synonyms]
  - carbuncular
[Antonyms]
  - unhealthy (indirect)
      -> healthy
  - adorned (indirect)
      -> unadorned
      -> undecorated
[Similar to]

  - decorated
