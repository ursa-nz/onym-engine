term: angry
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) feeling or showing anger
      "angry at the weather"
      "angry customers"
      "an angry silence"
      "sending angry letters to the papers"
  - (adjective) (of the elements) as if showing violent anger
      "angry clouds on the horizon"
      "furious winds"
      "the raging sea"
  - (adjective) severely inflamed and painful
      "an angry sore"
[Synonyms]
  - furious
  - raging
  - tempestuous
  - wild
[Antonyms]
  - unangry (direct)
  - stormy (indirect)
      -> calm
  - unhealthy (indirect)
      -> healthy
