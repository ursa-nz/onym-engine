term: conic morel
[Definitions]
  - (noun) a morel whose pitted fertile body is attached to the stalk with little free skirt around it; the fertile body is grey when young and black in old age
[Synonyms]
  - black morel
  - Morchella conica
  - Morchella angusticeps
  - narrowhead morel
