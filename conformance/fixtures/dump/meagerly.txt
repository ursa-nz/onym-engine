term: meagerly
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) deficient in amount or quality or extent
      "meager resources"
      "meager fare"
  - (adverb) to a meager degree or in a meager manner
      "these voices are meagerly represented at the conference"
      "the area is slenderly endowed with natural resources"
[Synonyms]
  - meager
  - meagre
  - stingy
  - scrimpy
  - sparingly
  - slenderly
  - meagrely
[Antonyms]
  - amply (direct)
      -> fully
      -> richly
[Similar to]
  - bare
  - scanty
  - spare
  - exiguous
  - hand-to-mouth
  - hardscrabble
  - measly
  - miserable
  - paltry
[Attributes]
  - sufficiency
  - adequacy
[Pertains to]
  - meager, meagre, stingy, scrimpy
