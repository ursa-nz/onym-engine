term: grilled
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) cooked by radiant heat (as over a grill)
  - (adjective) cooked over an outdoor grill
  - (verb) cook over or under a grill
      "grill the sausages"
  - (verb) examine thoroughly
      "the student was grilled for two hours on the subject of phonology"
[Synonyms]
  - broiled
  - barbecued
