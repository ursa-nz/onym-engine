term: freak
[Definitions]

  - (noun) someone who is so ardently devoted to something that it resembles an addiction
      "a golf addict"
      "a car nut"
      "a bodybuilding freak"
      "a news junkie"
  - (verb) lose one's nerve
      "When he saw the accident, he freaked out"
