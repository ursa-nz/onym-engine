term: baseborn
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) of low birth or station (`base' is archaic in this sense)
      "baseborn wretches with dirty faces"
      "of humble (or lowly) birth"
  - (adjective) illegitimate
[Synonyms]
  - base
  - humble
  - lowly
[Antonyms]
  - lowborn (indirect)
      -> noble
  - illegitimate (indirect)
      -> legitimate
[Similar to]
  - lowborn
  - illegitimate
[Domains]
  - archaism
  - archaicism
