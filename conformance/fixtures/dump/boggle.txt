term: boggle
[Definitions]
  - (verb) startle with amazement or fear
  - (verb) hesitate when confronted with a problem, or when in doubt or fear
  - (verb) overcome with amazement
      "This boggles the mind!"
[Synonyms]
  - flabbergast
  - bowl over
