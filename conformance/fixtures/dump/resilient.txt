term: resilient
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) recovering readily from adversity, depression, or the like
  - (adjective) elastic; rebounds readily
      "clean bouncy hair"
      "a lively tennis ball"
      "as resilient as seasoned hickory"
      "springy turf"
[Synonyms]
  - bouncy
  - live
  - lively
  - springy
[Antonyms]
  - spirited (indirect)
      -> spiritless
  - elastic (indirect)
      -> inelastic
[Derived forms]
  - resilience
  - resiliency
  - bounce
  - resile
  - take a hop
  - spring
  - bound
  - rebound
  - recoil
  - reverberate
  - ricochet
[Similar to]
  - spirited
  - elastic
