term: avant-garde
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) radically new or original
      "an avant-garde theater piece"
  - (noun) any creative group active in the innovation and application of new concepts and techniques in a given field (especially in the arts)
[Synonyms]
  - vanguard
  - van
  - new wave
  - daring
[Antonyms]
  - original (indirect)
      -> unoriginal
[Similar to]
  - original
[Is a kind of]
  - artistic movement, art movement
    - movement, social movement, front
      - social group
        - group, grouping
          - abstraction, abstract entity
            - entity
