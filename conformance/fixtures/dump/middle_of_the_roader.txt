term: middle-of-the-road
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) supporting or pursuing a course of action that is neither liberal nor conservative
  - (adjective) not extreme, especially in political views
  - (noun) a person who takes a position in the political center
[Synonyms]
  - centrist
  - moderate
  - moderationist
[Antonyms]
  - left (indirect)
      -> right
  - center (indirect)
      -> left
  - moderate (indirect)
      -> immoderate
[Similar to]
  - center
  - centre
  - moderate
