term: overcloud
[Definitions]
  - (verb) become covered with clouds
      "The sky clouded over"
  - (verb) make unintelligible or unclear
      "The distinction was obscured"
[Synonyms]
  - cloud over
  - cloud up
  - obscure
  - bedim

  - change, alter, modify
