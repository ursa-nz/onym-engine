term: unsound
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) not in good condition; damaged or decayed
      "an unsound foundation"
  - (adjective) not sound financially
      "unsound banking practices"
  - (adjective) containing or based on a fallacy
      "fallacious reasoning"
      "an unsound argument"
  - (adjective) suffering from severe mental illness
      "of unsound mind"
  - (adjective) physically unsound or diseased
      "has a bad back"
      "a bad heart"
      "bad teeth"
      "an unsound limb"
      "unsound teeth"
  - (adjective) of e.g. advice
[Synonyms]
