term: well-balanced
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) in an optimal state of balance or equilibrium
      "a well-balanced wheel"
  - (adjective) free from psychological disorder
      "a well-adjusted personality"
[Synonyms]
  - well-adjusted
[Antonyms]
  - balanced (indirect)
      -> unbalanced
      -> imbalanced
  - adjusted (indirect)
      -> maladjusted
[Similar to]
  - balanced
  - adjusted
