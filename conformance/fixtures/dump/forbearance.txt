term: forbearance
[Definitions]
  - (noun) good-natured tolerance of delay or incompetence
  - (noun) a delay in enforcing rights or claims or privileges; refraining from acting
      "his forbearance to reply was alarming"
[Synonyms]
  - patience
  - longanimity
