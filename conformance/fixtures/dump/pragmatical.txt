term: pragmatical
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) of or concerning the theory of pragmatism
  - (adjective) concerned with practical matters
      "a matter-of-fact (or pragmatic) approach to the problem"
      "a matter-of-fact account of the trip"
[Synonyms]
  - pragmatic
  - matter-of-fact
[Antonyms]
  - practical (indirect)
      -> impractical
[Derived forms]
  - pragmatism
  - realism
[Similar to]
  - practical
[Pertains to]
  - pragmatism
    - philosophical doctrine, philosophical theory
