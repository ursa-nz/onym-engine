term: het
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) made warm or hot (‘het’ is a dialectal variant of ‘heated’)
      "a heated swimming pool"
      "wiped his heated-up face with a large bandana"
      "he was all het up and sweaty"
[Synonyms]
  - heated
  - heated up
  - het up
[Antonyms]
  - hot (indirect)
      -> cold
[Similar to]
  - hot
