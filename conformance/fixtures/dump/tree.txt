term: tree
[Definitions]
  - (noun) a tall perennial woody plant having a main trunk and branches forming a distinct elevated crown; includes both gymnosperms and angiosperms
  - (noun) a figure that branches from a single root
      "genealogical tree"
  - (noun) English actor and theatrical producer noted for his lavish productions of Shakespeare (1853-1917)
  - (verb) force a person or an animal into a position from which they cannot escape
  - (verb) plant with trees
      "this lot should be treed so that the house will be shaded in summer"
  - (verb) chase an animal up a tree
      "the hunters treed the bear with dogs and killed it"
      "her dog likes to tree squirrels"
  - (verb) stretch (a shoe) on a shoetree
