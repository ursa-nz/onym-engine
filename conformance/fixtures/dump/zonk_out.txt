term: zonk out
[Definitions]
  - (verb) lose consciousness due to a sudden trauma, for example
  - (verb) fall asleep fast, as when one is extremely tired
      "after the long drive, we zonked out and slept for 10 hours"
[Synonyms]
  - pass out
  - black out
[Is a kind of]
  - change state, turn
    - change
      - act, move
  - fall asleep, dope off, flake out, drift off, nod off, drop off, doze off, drowse off
    - change, alter, vary
      - act, move
[Kinds]
  - faint, conk, swoon, pass out
