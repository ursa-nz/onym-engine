term: hard-line
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) firm and uncompromising
      "a hard-line policy"
  - (noun) a firm and uncompromising stance or position
      "the governor took a hard line on drugs"
[Antonyms]
  - uncompromising (indirect)
      -> compromising
      -> conciliatory
      -> flexible
[Similar to]
  - uncompromising
  - sturdy
  - inflexible
[Is a kind of]
  - position, stance, posture
    - attitude, mental attitude
      - cognition, knowledge, noesis
        - psychological feature
          - abstraction, abstract entity
            - entity
