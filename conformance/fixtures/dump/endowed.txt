term: endowed
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) provided or supplied or equipped with (especially as by inheritance or nature)
      "a well-endowed college"
      "endowed with good eyesight"
      "endowed by their Creator with certain unalienable rights"
  - (verb) give qualities or abilities to
  - (verb) furnish with an endowment
      "When she got married, she got dowered"
[Synonyms]
  - indue
  - gift
  - empower
  - invest
  - endue
  - dower
[Antonyms]
  - unendowed (direct)
      -> dowerless
      -> unblessed
[Derived forms]

  - gift
  - talent
  - natural endowment
  - endowment fund
[Similar to]
