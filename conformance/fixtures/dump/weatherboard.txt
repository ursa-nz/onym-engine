term: weatherboard
[Definitions]
  - (noun) a long thin board with one edge thicker than the other; used as siding by lapping one board over the board below
  - (noun) the side toward the wind
[Synonyms]
  - clapboard
  - weatherboarding
  - windward side
  - weather side
