term: commensurate
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) corresponding in size or degree or extent
      "pay should be commensurate with the time worked"
[Antonyms]
  - incommensurate (direct)
      -> disproportionate
      -> incommensurable
[Derived forms]
  - commensurateness
  - correspondence
  - proportionateness
