term: terrestrial dynamical time
[Definitions]
  - (noun) (astronomy) a measure of time defined by Earth's orbital motion; terrestrial time is mean solar time corrected for the irregularities of the Earth's motions
[Synonyms]
  - terrestrial time
  - TT
  - TDT
  - ephemeris time
[Is a kind of]
