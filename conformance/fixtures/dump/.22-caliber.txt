term: .22-caliber
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) of or relating to the bore of a gun (or its ammunition) that measures twenty-two hundredths of an inch in diameter
      "a .22 caliber pistol"
[Synonyms]
  - .22 calibre
  - .22-calibre
[Pertains to]
  - bore, gauge, caliber, calibre
    - diameter, diam
