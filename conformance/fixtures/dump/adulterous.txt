term: adulterous
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) characterized by adultery
      "an adulterous relationship"
      "extramarital affairs"
      "the extracurricular activities of a philandering husband"
  - (adjective) not faithful to a spouse or lover
      "adulterous husbands and wives"
      "a two-timing boyfriend"
[Synonyms]
  - extramarital
  - extracurricular
  - cheating
  - two-timing
