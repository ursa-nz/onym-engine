term: slosh
[Definitions]
  - (verb) make a splashing sound
      "water was splashing on the floor"
  - (verb) walk through mud or mire
      "We had to splosh across the wet meadow"
  - (verb) spill or splash copiously or clumsily
      "slosh paint all over the walls"
[Synonyms]
  - splash
  - splosh
  - slush
  - squelch
  - squish
  - slop
  - slosh around
  - slush around
