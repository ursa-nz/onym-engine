term: spick-and-span
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) conspicuously new
      "shiny brand-new shoes"
      "a spick-and-span novelty"
  - (adjective) completely neat and clean
      "the apartment was immaculate"
      "in her immaculate white uniform"
      "a spick-and-span kitchen"
      "their spic red-visored caps"
[Synonyms]
  - brand-new
  - bran-new
  - spic-and-span
  - immaculate
  - speckless
  - spic
  - spick
  - spotless
[Antonyms]
  - new (indirect)
      -> old
  - clean (indirect)
      -> dirty
      -> soiled
      -> unclean
[Similar to]
  - new
  - clean
