term: faultfinding
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) tending to make moral judgments or judgments based on personal opinions
      "a counselor tries not to be faultfinding"
  - (adjective) tending to find and call attention to faults
      "a captious pedant"
      "an excessively demanding and faultfinding tutor"
  - (noun) persistent petty and unjustified criticism
[Synonyms]
  - carping
  - captious
[Antonyms]
  - judgmental (indirect)
      -> nonjudgmental
  - critical (indirect)
      -> uncritical
[Derived forms]
  - captious
  - carping
[Similar to]
  - judgmental
  - critical
[Is a kind of]
  - criticism, unfavorable judgment
    - disapproval
      - message, content, subject matter, substance
        - communication
          - abstraction, abstract entity
            - entity
