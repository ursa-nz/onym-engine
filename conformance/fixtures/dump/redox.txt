term: redox
[Definitions]
  - (noun) a reversible chemical reaction in which one reaction is an oxidation and the reverse is a reduction
[Synonyms]
  - oxidation-reduction
  - oxidoreduction
[Is a kind of]
  - chemical reaction, reaction
    - chemical process, chemical change, chemical action
      - natural process, natural action, action, activity
        - process, physical process
          - physical entity
            - entity
