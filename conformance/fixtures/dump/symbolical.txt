term: symbolical
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) relating to or using or proceeding by means of symbols
      "symbolic logic"
      "symbolic operations"
      "symbolic thinking"
  - (adjective) serving as a visible symbol for something abstract
      "a crown is emblematic of royalty"
      "the spinning wheel was as symbolic of colonical Massachusetts as the codfish"
[Synonyms]
  - symbolic
  - emblematic
  - emblematical
[Antonyms]
  - representative (indirect)
      -> nonrepresentative
      -> unsymbolic
[Derived forms]
  - symbol
  - symbolization
  - symbolisation
  - symbolic representation
[Similar to]
  - representative
[Pertains to]
  - symbol
    - signal, signaling, sign
