term: credibly
[Definitions]
  - (adverb) easy to believe on the basis of available evidence
      "he talked plausibly before the committee"
      "he will probably win the election"
[Synonyms]
  - believably
  - plausibly
  - probably
[Antonyms]
  - incredibly (direct)
      -> improbably
      -> implausibly
      -> unbelievably
[Pertains to]
  - credible, believable
