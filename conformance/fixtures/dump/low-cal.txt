term: low-cal
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) having relatively few calories
      "diet cola"
      "light (or lite) beer"
      "lite (or light) mayonnaise"
      "a low-cal diet"
[Synonyms]
  - light
  - lite
  - calorie-free
[Antonyms]
  - nonfat (indirect)
      -> fatty
      -> fat
[Similar to]
  - nonfat
  - fat-free
  - fatless
