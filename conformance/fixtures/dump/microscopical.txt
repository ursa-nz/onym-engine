term: microscopical
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) of or relating to or used in microscopy
      "microscopic analysis"
      "microscopical examination"
  - (adjective) visible under a microscope; using a microscope
  - (adjective) so small as to be invisible without a microscope
      "differences were microscopic"
[Synonyms]
  - microscopic
[Antonyms]
  - visible (indirect)
      -> invisible
      -> unseeable
  - small (indirect)
      -> large
      -> big
[Derived forms]
  - microscopy
  - microscope
[Similar to]
  - visible
  - seeable
  - small
  - little
[Pertains to]
  - microscopy
    - research
