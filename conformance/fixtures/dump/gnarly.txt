term: gnarly
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) used of old persons or old trees; covered with knobs or knots
      "gnarled and knotted hands"
      "a knobbed stick"
[Synonyms]
  - gnarled
  - knotted
  - knotty
  - knobbed
[Antonyms]
  - crooked (indirect)
      -> straight
[Derived forms]
  - knot
  - gnarl
[Similar to]
  - crooked
