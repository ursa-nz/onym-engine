term: boned
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) having had the bones removed
      "a boneless rib roast"
      "a boned (or deboned) fish"
  - (adjective) having bones as specified
      "his lanky long-boned body"
  - (verb) study intensively, as before an exam
      "I had to bone up on my Latin verbs before the final exam"
  - (verb) remove the bones from
      "bone the turkey before roasting it"
