term: ambidextrous
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) equally skillful with each hand
      "an ambidextrous surgeon"
  - (adjective) marked by deliberate deceptiveness especially by pretending one set of feelings and acting under the influence of another
      "she was a deceitful scheming little thing"
      "a double-dealing double agent"
      "a double-faced infernal traitor and schemer"
[Synonyms]
  - two-handed
  - deceitful
  - double-dealing
  - duplicitous
  - Janus-faced
  - two-faced
  - double-faced
  - double-tongued
[Antonyms]
  - right-handed (direct)
      -> dextral
      -> right
      -> right-hand
  - left-handed (direct)
      -> left
      -> left-hand
      -> sinistral
  - dishonest (indirect)
      -> honest
      -> honorable
[Derived forms]
  - ambidexterity
  - ambidextrousness
[Similar to]
  - equipoised
  - dishonest
  - dishonorable
