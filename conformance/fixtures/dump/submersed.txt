term: submersed
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) beneath the surface of the water
      "submerged rocks"
  - (adjective) growing or remaining under water
      "viewing subaqueous fauna from a glass-bottomed boat"
      "submerged leaves"
  - (verb) sink below the surface; go under or as if under water
  - (verb) put under water
      "submerge your head completely"
