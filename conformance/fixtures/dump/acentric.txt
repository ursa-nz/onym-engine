term: acentric
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) lacking a centromere
      "an acentric chromosome fragment"
  - (adjective) not centered or having no center
[Antonyms]
  - eccentric (indirect)
      -> concentric
      -> concentrical
      -> homocentric
[Similar to]
  - eccentric
  - nonconcentric
[Pertains to]
  - centromere, kinetochore
    - structure, anatomical structure, complex body part, bodily structure, body structure
