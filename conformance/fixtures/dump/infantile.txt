term: infantile
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) indicating a lack of maturity
      "childish tantrums"
      "infantile behavior"
  - (adjective) of or relating to infants or infancy
      "infantile paralysis"
  - (adjective) being or befitting or characteristic of an infant
      "infantile games"
[Synonyms]
  - childish
[Antonyms]
  - immature (indirect)
      -> mature
  - young (indirect)
      -> old
[Similar to]
  - immature
  - young
[Pertains to]
  - infancy, babyhood, early childhood
    - time of life
[Domains]
  - smol
