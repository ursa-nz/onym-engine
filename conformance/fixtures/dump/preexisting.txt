term: preexisting
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) existing previously or before something
      "variations on pre-existent musical themes"
  - (verb) exist beforehand or prior to a certain point in time
      "Did this condition pre-exist?"
[Synonyms]
  - preexistent
  - pre-existent
  - pre-existing
[Antonyms]
  - antecedent (indirect)
      -> subsequent
[Derived forms]
  - preexistent
  - pre-existent
  - preexisting
  - pre-existing
  - preexistence
[Similar to]
  - antecedent
[Is a kind of]
  - exist, be
