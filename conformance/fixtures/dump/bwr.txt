term: BWR
[Definitions]
  - (noun) a nuclear reactor that uses water as a coolant and moderator; the water boils in the reactor core and the steam produced can drive a steam turbine
[Synonyms]
  - boiling water reactor
[Is a kind of]
  - water-cooled reactor
