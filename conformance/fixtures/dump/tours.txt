term: tour
[Definitions]
  - (noun) a journey or route all the way around a particular place or area
      "they took an extended tour of Europe"
      "we took a quick circuit of the park"
      "a ten-day coach circuit of the island"
  - (noun) a time period for working (after which you will be relieved by someone else)
      "it's my go"
      "a spell of work"
  - (noun) a period of time spent in military service
  - (verb) make a tour of a certain place
      "We toured the Provence this summer"
  - (noun) an industrial city in western France on the Loire River
