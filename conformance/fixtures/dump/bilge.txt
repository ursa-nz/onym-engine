term: bilge
[Definitions]
  - (noun) water accumulated in the bilge of a ship
  - (noun) where the sides of the vessel curve in to form the bottom
  - (verb) cause to leak
      "the collision bilged the vessel"
  - (verb) take in water at the bilge
      "the tanker bilged"
