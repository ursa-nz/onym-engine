term: astronomic
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) relating or belonging to the science of astronomy
      "astronomic telescope"
  - (adjective) inconceivably large
[Synonyms]
  - astronomical
  - galactic
[Antonyms]
  - large (indirect)
      -> small
      -> little
[Derived forms]
  - astronomy
  - uranology
[Similar to]
  - large
  - big
[Pertains to]
  - astronomy, uranology
    - physics, natural philosophy
