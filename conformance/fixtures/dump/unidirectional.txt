term: unidirectional
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) operating or moving or allowing movement in one direction only
      "a unidirectional flow"
      "a unidirectional antenna"
      "a unidirectional approach to a problem"
[Antonyms]
  - bidirectional (direct)
      -> biface
      -> bifacial
      -> duplex
      -> two-way
[Similar to]
  - one-way
  - simplex
  - unifacial
