term: rustle
[Definitions]
  - (verb) make a dry crackling sound
      "rustling silk"
      "the dry leaves were rustling in the breeze"
  - (verb) take illegally
      "rustle cattle"
  - (verb) forage food
  - (noun) a light noise, like the noise of silk clothing or leaves blowing in the wind
