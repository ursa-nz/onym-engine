term: aah
[Definitions]
  - (verb) express admiration and pleasure by uttering `ooh' or `aah'
      "They oohed and aahed when they unwrapped the presents"
[Synonyms]
  - ooh
[Is a kind of]
  - exclaim, cry, cry out, outcry, call out, shout
    - express, verbalize, verbalise, utter, give tongue to
