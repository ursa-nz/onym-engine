term: penultimate
[Definitions]
  - (noun) the next to last syllable in a word
  - (adjective) second last
      "the author inadvertently reveals the murderer in the penultimate chapter"
      "the figures in the next-to-last column"
[Synonyms]

  - penultima
  - next-to-last
