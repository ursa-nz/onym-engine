term: efficacy
[Definitions]
  - (noun) capacity or power to produce a desired effect
      "concern about the safety and efficacy of the vaccine"
[Synonyms]
  - efficaciousness
[Antonyms]
  - inefficacy (direct)
      -> inefficaciousness

  - effectual
