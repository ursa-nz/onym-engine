term: unanalyzable
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) representing the furthest possible extent of analysis or division into parts
      "a feeling is a simple and undecomposable mental state"
      "this weight of evidence is something mystical and unanalyzable"
[Synonyms]
  - undecomposable
[Antonyms]
  - simple (indirect)
      -> complex
[Similar to]
  - simple
