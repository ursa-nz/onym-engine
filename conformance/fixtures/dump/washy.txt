term: washy
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) overly diluted; thin and insipid
      "washy coffee"
      "watery milk"
      "weak tea"
  - (adjective) having lost freshness or brilliance of color
      "sun-bleached deck chairs"
      "faded jeans"
      "a very pale washed-out blue"
      "washy colors"
[Synonyms]
  - watery
  - weak
  - bleached
  - faded
  - washed-out
[Antonyms]
  - diluted (indirect)
      -> undiluted
  - colorless (indirect)
      -> colorful
      -> colourful
[Similar to]
  - diluted
  - dilute
  - colorless
  - colourless
