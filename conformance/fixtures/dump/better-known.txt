term: better-known
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) more familiar or renowned than the other of two
      "the better-known book of the two"
  - (adjective) widely or fully known
      "a well-known politician"
      "well-known facts"
      "a politician who is well known"
      "these facts are well known"
  - (adjective) frequently experienced; known closely or intimately
      "a long-familiar face"
      "a well-known voice reached her ears"
[Synonyms]
  - long-familiar
[Antonyms]
  - known (indirect)
      -> unknown
  - familiar (indirect)
      -> unfamiliar
[Similar to]
  - known
  - familiar
