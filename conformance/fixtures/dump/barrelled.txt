term: barrelled
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) put in or stored in a barrel
      "barreled beer"
  - (adjective) (of an arrow) tapered toward both ends
  - (verb) put in barrels
[Synonyms]
  - barreled
[Antonyms]
  - pointed (indirect)
      -> pointless
      -> unpointed
[Derived forms]

  - cask
[Similar to]
