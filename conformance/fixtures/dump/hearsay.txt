term: hearsay
[Definitions]
  - (noun) gossip (usually a mixture of truth and untruth) passed around by word of mouth
  - (adjective) heard through another rather than directly
      "hearsay information"
[Synonyms]
  - rumor
  - rumour
[Antonyms]
  - indirect (indirect)
      -> direct
[Similar to]
  - indirect
[Is a kind of]
  - gossip, comment, scuttlebutt
    - report, account
      - informing, making known
        - speech act
          - act, deed, human action, human activity
            - event
              - abstraction, abstract entity
                - entity
