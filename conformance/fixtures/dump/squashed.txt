term: squashed
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) that has been violently compressed
      "the squashed looking nakedness of the fledgling birds"
  - (verb) to compress with violence, out of natural shape or condition
      "crush an aluminum can"
      "squeeze a lemon"
[Synonyms]
  - crush
  - squelch
  - mash
  - squeeze
