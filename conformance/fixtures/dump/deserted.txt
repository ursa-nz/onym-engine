term: deserted
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) forsaken by owner or inhabitants
      "weed-grown yard of an abandoned farmhouse"
  - (verb) leave someone who needs or counts on you; leave in the lurch
      "The mother deserted her children"
  - (verb) desert (a cause, a country or an army), often in order to join the opposing cause, country, or army
      "If soldiers deserted Hitler's army, they were shot"
  - (verb) leave behind
      "the students deserted the campus after the end of exam period"
