term: akin
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) similar in quality or character
      "a feeling akin to terror"
      "kindred souls"
  - (adjective) related by blood
[Synonyms]
  - kindred
  - blood-related
  - cognate
  - consanguine
  - consanguineous
  - consanguineal
  - kin
[Antonyms]
  - similar (indirect)
      -> dissimilar
  - related (indirect)
      -> unrelated
[Similar to]
  - similar
  - related
