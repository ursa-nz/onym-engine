term: drizzle
[Definitions]
  - (noun) very light rain; stronger than mist but less than a shower
  - (verb) rain lightly
      "When it drizzles in summer, hiking can be pleasant"
  - (verb) moisten with fine drops
      "drizzle the meat with melted butter"
[Synonyms]
  - mizzle
  - moisten
