term: non-poisonous
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) not producing poison
  - (adjective) safe to eat
[Synonyms]
  - nontoxic
[Antonyms]
  - nontoxic (indirect)
      -> toxic
  - edible (indirect)
      -> inedible
      -> uneatable
[Similar to]
  - nontoxic
  - atoxic
  - edible
  - comestible
  - eatable
