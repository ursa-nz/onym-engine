term: mousy
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) quiet and timid and ineffectual
  - (adjective) infested with mice
  - (adjective) of something having a drab pale brown color resembling a mouse
      "a mousy brownish-grey color"
      "mousy hair"
      "mouse-colored hair"
[Synonyms]
  - mousey
  - mouse-colored
  - mouselike
[Antonyms]
  - timid (indirect)
      -> bold
  - chromatic (indirect)
      -> achromatic
      -> neutral
[Derived forms]
  - mouse
[Similar to]
  - timid
  - chromatic
[Pertains to]
  - mouse
    - rodent, gnawer
