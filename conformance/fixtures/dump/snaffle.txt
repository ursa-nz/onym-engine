term: snaffle
[Definitions]
  - (verb) get hold of or seize quickly and easily
      "I snapped up all the good buys during the garage sale"
  - (verb) fit or restrain with a snaffle
      "snaffle a horse"
  - (noun) a simple jointed bit for a horse; without a curb
