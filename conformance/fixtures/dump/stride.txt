term: stride
[Definitions]
  - (noun) a step in walking or running
  - (noun) the distance covered by a step
      "he stepped off ten paces from the old tree and began to dig"
  - (noun) significant progress (especially in the phrase ‘make strides’)
      "they made big strides in productivity"
  - (verb) walk with long steps
      "He strode confidently across the hall"
