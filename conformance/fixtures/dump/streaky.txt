term: streaky
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) marked with or as if with stripes or linear discolorations
      "streaked hair"
      "fat legs and dirty streaky faces"
[Synonyms]
  - streaked
[Antonyms]
  - patterned (indirect)
      -> plain
      -> unpatterned
[Derived forms]
  - stripe
  - streak
  - bar
[Similar to]
  - patterned
