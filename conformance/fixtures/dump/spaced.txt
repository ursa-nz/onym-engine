term: spaced
[Definitions]
  - (adjective) spaced apart
  - (adjective) arranged with spaces between; often used as a combining form
      "widely spaced eyes"
  - (verb) place at intervals
      "Space the interviews so that you have some time between the different candidates"
[Synonyms]
  - separated
[Antonyms]
  - distributed (indirect)
      -> concentrated
  - unspaced (direct)
      -> unleaded
[Derived forms]
  - distance
  - blank
  - spacing
  - spatial arrangement
[Similar to]
  - distributed
  - double-spaced
  - leaded
  - single-spaced

      - change, alter, modify
        - induce, stimulate, cause, have, get, make
          - act, move
