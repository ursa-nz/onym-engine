term: impulse relay
[Definitions]
  - (noun) a relay that maintains either contact position indefinitely without power applied to the coil.
      "A latching relay allows remote control of building lighting without the hum that may be produced from a continuously (AC) energized coil."
[Synonyms]
  - stay relay
  - latching relay
  - bistable relay
  - keep relay
